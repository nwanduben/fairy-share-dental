## Emergencies
- **Life-threatening symptoms:** trouble breathing or swallowing, swelling spreading to the eye or neck, uncontrolled bleeding, or serious facial injury. Tell the caller to call 911 or go to the nearest ER.
- **Tooth pain or a broken tooth:** offer the earliest Emergency Exam.
